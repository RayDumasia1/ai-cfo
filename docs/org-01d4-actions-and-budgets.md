# ORG-01D4 actions, audit and organization budgets

31 October 2026. **D4A inventory and implementation requirements are complete. Storage repair and authority conversion are not installed.** The owner requested D4 development alongside D3 validation. D3 remains in preview awaiting hosted owner acceptance; its production SQL and promotion are separate release gates. [D sequence](org-01d-financial-ownership-conversion.md), [organization architecture](org-01-organization-architecture.md), [backlog](backlog.md).

## Outcome and boundaries

D4 moves action records, action history and usage budgets to verified organization ownership while keeping current behavior and counts. Original owners remain the only enabled actors. No action ranking/sharing redesign, team assignment, invitation, seat packaging, new subscription, Stripe customer change or AI launch is included. ACTION-01 holds the owner's future dashboard requirements. QuickBooks remains parked. D5 still owns the complete weekly graph conversion.

Implement D4 in two further reviewed units after this preflight: **D4B storage and explicit legacy-link repair**, then **D4C authority, capacity and usage cutover**. A new ownership column alone is not authorization. D4B must not claim that collaboration or shared-user budgets are enabled; D4C must close all alternate writers before claiming that boundary. Starting D4 does not imply D3 owner acceptance or production installation.

## D4B storage and repair requirements

Add non-null `organization_id` to `actions`, `action_audit_log` and `usage_tracking`. Use the verified D2 private scope as the stable compatibility identity, with organization/legacy-owner FKs and organization-qualified action/history parent constraints. Introduce organization/metric/period uniqueness while retaining the existing user/metric/period key during original-owner compatibility. Do not create per-member allowance buckets or copy rows into a second budget. D5 will add organization-qualified forecast-parent keys; until then retain the existing forecast owner/item/version FKs and explicitly validate header business ownership too.

Only null action business links may be repaired, to the unique profile proven by that action's legacy owner, live original mapping and D2 immutable scope. An existing non-null wrong link, missing/detached mapping, foreign history/forecast lineage or ambiguous evidence aborts the whole install. Never reassign a conflicting row to make a foreign key pass. Record each actual null-link correction in private immutable migration evidence, with original/new references and the real correction time/provenance. This is administrative scope correction, not a historical customer action or inferred `created_by`.

Take locks that exclude concurrent target writes and relevant lifecycle/mapping changes before preflight and comparison. Preserve action IDs, names/descriptions, goal fields, status, completion dates, due dates, alerts, forecast/item references and original timestamps. Existing actions have `actions_set_updated_at`, `actions_completed_at_trigger` and `enforce_action_capacity` triggers. Review their actual definitions/digests; if the timestamp trigger must be temporarily disabled for this checked backfill, disable only that known trigger transactionally, restore it, and compare every old field after the change. Abort on unexpected triggers rather than silently disabling them. Check all public row values, except precisely documented new scope columns and the proven null-to-profile repairs. No profile/data version, financial amount, billing or action-capacity change is authorized by the repair.

Future legacy INSERTs need strict scope stamping and owner/profile immutability guards so the old create helper's nullable business input cannot keep generating unscoped actions. Audits inherit the action's verified organization and legacy owner; a matching `user_id` alone cannot attach history to another owner's action. Usage rows inherit the checked owner scope. Preserve zero/null distinctions, period keys, count, limit, warning timestamp and created/updated timestamps; no counter reset, sum, rounding or age-based deletion. Keep existing owner RLS and API behavior during D4B, with a clear statement that write authority is still D4C work.

Profile deletion currently sets action business links to null. Preserve stable organization evidence and test the legitimate deletion path without allowing a normal caller to detach or reassign a live action. Auth deletion currently cascades actions/history, but the observed usage-owner FK has default NO ACTION, not CASCADE. Preserve and test that distinction: a populated usage row may block Auth deletion atomically. Do not silently change retention or delete counters to make the test pass; D6 must reconcile lifecycle policy before collaboration. Future attributable events must not rely solely on the deletable legacy status-history table.

## D4C authoritative operations and locking

Convert create, edit, complete, reopen, delete and status audit together. Cookie-authenticated operations bind `auth.uid()` and an optional single selected organization; request actor/owner/tier/limit fields cannot grant access. Resolve current original-owner mapping/revisions under locks. Close direct browser/service action DML, raw history INSERT, the legacy update RPC and any compatibility aliases at cutover. Keep a checked SELECT path for action lists/dashboard/history and the unconverted draft-discard dependency. Capture newly verified actors in private immutable operation evidence atomically with changed actions, including deletion, without relabeling old audit owners as historical authors.

Use the established organization root → current Auth/membership → profile → subscription/capacity → action/usage/forecast references order. Use bounded NOWAIT on legacy predecessor dependencies as appropriate, recheck after waiting for the root, and require fresh READ COMMITTED snapshots. D3's monthly helper is a financial capability; do not mislabel action or quota authority as `financial.months.write`. Share the proven lock mechanics only through a reviewed capability-specific resolver. Existing subscription-first capacity triggers, action-first update paths, Auth/profile deletion and version-first draft discard must be included in independent-session tests. Do not acquire an earlier root from inside a trigger after a legacy writer already holds a later resource.

Preserve active-action limits: Starter 10, Core 50, Growth/Advisory unlimited, suspended 0 new slots. Completion/deletion and edits that take no new active slot must retain their existing remedial behavior when an owner is above a downgraded limit. Reopening consumes a slot. New-slot authorization additionally checks current eligible target coverage; a stale actor subscription or browser-supplied tier is insufficient. Lock and count the organization action set atomically with the authoritative current subscription. Two requests competing for the last slot must produce one admission and one dedicated capacity denial, without partial action/history writes. Concurrent downgrade cannot leave a stale higher allowance in use.

Quota admission derives metric eligibility, period and finite/unlimited policy from the current target subscription under locks. Do not accept arbitrary client limits, dates or overage flags, and do not substitute the current time for a missing billing anchor on each request. Preserve existing periods rather than silently rebucketing them. Existing finite limits are Core 20 Ask CFO / 3 insights and Growth 150 Ask CFO with existing fair-use overage; unlimited metrics remain untracked. Denial consumes no unit. Allowed overage increments once and reports throttling. Existing warning state survives conversion; delivery/marking remains ancillary and must not undo a committed counter. Trusted background jobs need a separately validated capability, not a generic service-role owner override. The legacy `consume_usage`, disabled `increment_usage`, raw service counter/mark-warning updates and `usageGate` helper must all be accounted for at cutover.

Ask CFO and AI Insights still return `501 FEATURE_NOT_IMPLEMENTED` before usage work. D4 must not wire in consumption, email warnings, provider calls or new costs for unavailable features. New AI execution/replay/refund semantics belong to their later delivery increment. Stripe identities, billing webhooks, product prices, sales flags and commercial limits are unchanged.
